# Text-only media policy

User decision 2026-11-24: exclude video, audio, podcasts, radio, BBC Sounds and iPlayer. Do not collect transcripts or media pages. Keep raw RSS responses as discovery provenance, but do not insert new media article rows. Existing records and analysis commitments are immutable; report them separately and exclude them from new text analysis and market evaluation. Genuine short factual text and live text articles remain in scope. This policy is not a claim that all important events are necessarily reported in text.
